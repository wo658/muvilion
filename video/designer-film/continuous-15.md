# Continuous 15-second motion study

Composition: Muvilion-Continuous-15. 450 frames at30fps,1920×1080.

0–3.7s: three source cards enter, camera sweeps and rolls; the same cards shrink into attachments.
3.7–7.5s: central composer holds for a readable typed prompt.
7.5–9.8s: send press, camera dive and pullback; composer outline becomes blueprint.
9.8–15s: saved layout geometry lifts into volume; camera settles on the result.

Editorial motion study, not real-time app recording or a newly executed generation. Layout uses the saved16-object Community Studio design; source-card thumbnails are schematic. The existing60-second composition remains available. Sound effects are synthesized locally with seeded noise and sine tones; no third-party samples.
