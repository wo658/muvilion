# Designer advertisement assets

`layout.json` is a geometry-only extraction of the actual saved English Community Studio project (`designer-film/generated/minute-final-project.json`). Its installation positions, sizes, rotations and elevations drive the stylized SVG build in `DesignerAdSpace.tsx`. Materials are mapped to repository tokens. It is an editorial construction animation, not footage of live computational steps or a surveyed venue.

`space-orbit.mp4` crops the actual `designer-minute/orbit.mp4` recording to x110/y70/1080×600. The crop removes app controls and the mascot launcher. `space-frame.jpg` is the frame at 5.7 seconds of this cropped recording. The camera frame and freeze/photo-card transition are editorial effects, not evidence of a new app photography request.

No earlier project's concept photos are used.
